# Invoice Ninja Features

Invoice Ninja is a powerful open-source platform designed to help freelancers and businesses of all sizes manage their invoicing, payments, expenses, and projects. Below is a comprehensive list of its key features, categorized for clarity.

## I. Invoicing & Billing

*   **Create & Manage Professional Invoices:** Easily generate, customize, and track professional invoices. Send them to your clients via email and get paid faster.
    *   *Design with Pre-built Templates:* Choose from a variety of templates to create visually appealing invoices.
    *   *Customize with Your Logo & Branding:* Reinforce your brand by adding your logo and company details.
    *   *Support for Multiple Currencies:* Bill clients in their local currency.
    *   *Automatic Tax Calculation:* Configure and apply tax rates accurately.
    *   *Line Item Discounts & Totals:* Clearly present pricing, discounts, and totals.
    *   *Due Dates & Payment Terms:* Set clear expectations for payment.
*   **Automate Billing with Recurring Invoices:** Set up recurring profiles to automatically bill clients for ongoing services, saving you time and ensuring consistent cash flow.
    *   *Flexible Scheduling:* Define billing frequency (daily, weekly, monthly, yearly).
    *   *Auto-Bill & Auto-Pay Options:* Enable automatic payment collection for approved clients.
*   **Send Professional Quotes & Proposals:** Create and send detailed quotes or proposals to potential clients.
    *   *Convert Quotes to Invoices:* Easily convert an approved quote into an invoice with a single click.
    *   *Quote Expiration Tracking:* Set validity periods for your quotes.
*   **Issue Credit Notes & Manage Refunds:** Efficiently create credit notes for returns or adjustments and manage the refund process.
*   *Track Invoice Status:* Know at a glance whether invoices are draft, sent, viewed, partially paid, paid, or overdue.
*   *Generate Delivery Notes:* Create and send delivery notes for shipped goods.
*   *Download Invoices/Quotes/Credits as PDF:* Provide clients with universally accessible PDF documents.
*   *E-invoicing Compliance (select regions):* Generate invoices in standard electronic formats for compliance with local regulations (e.g., Peppol).

## II. Client Management

*   **Centralized Client Database:** Manage all your client information in one place – from contact details to communication history.
    *   *Detailed Client Records:* Store addresses, contact persons, VAT ID, and custom notes.
    *   *Client Activity Feed:* View a history of all interactions and transactions with a client.
*   **Secure Client Portal:** Offer your clients a dedicated portal to view their invoices, quotes, payment history, and approve quotes online.
    *   *Client Self-Service:* Clients can download their documents and view account statements.
*   *Client Groups:* Organize your clients into groups for easier management and reporting.
*   *Document Management:* Attach important files and documents directly to client records.
*   *Merge Duplicate Clients:* Keep your client list clean and accurate by merging duplicate entries.
*   *VAT Number Validation:* Assist with EU VAT compliance by validating client VAT numbers.

## III. Payments

*   **Online Payment Gateway Integration:** Connect with popular payment gateways (e.g., Stripe, PayPal) to accept online payments directly on your invoices.
    *   *Accept Credit/Debit Cards:* Allow clients to pay using major credit and debit cards.
    *   *ACH/Bank Transfer Support:* Offer direct bank transfer payment options.
*   **Record & Track Payments:** Manually record offline payments (cash, check, bank transfer) and link them to invoices.
    *   *Partial Payments:* Record and manage partial payments for invoices.
*   *Payment Notifications:* Receive and send automatic notifications for successful payments.
*   *Securely Store Client Payment Methods:* Allow clients to securely save their payment details for faster future payments (via gateway tokenization).
*   *Refund Processing:* Manage refunds directly through integrated payment gateways where supported.

## IV. Product & Service Management

*   **Product & Service Library:** Create a library of your products and services for quick addition to invoices and quotes.
    *   *Detailed Item Descriptions:* Include notes, cost, and quantity.
    *   *Standard Pricing:* Set default prices for your items.
*   *Document Attachments:* Attach relevant documents or images to product/service entries.
*   *Tax Configuration per Item:* Apply specific tax rates to different products or services if needed.

## V. Expense Tracking

*   **Record & Categorize Business Expenses:** Track your business expenses to get a clear picture of your spending.
    *   *Attach Receipts & Documents:* Keep digital copies of your receipts linked to expenses.
    *   *Recurring Expenses:* Set up recurring profiles for regular expenses like subscriptions.
*   *Billable Expenses:* Easily mark expenses as billable to a client and add them to invoices.
*   *E-Document Import for Expenses:* Streamline expense entry by importing electronic receipts/invoices where available.

## VI. Project & Task Management

*   **Create & Manage Projects:** Organize your work by creating projects, assigning them to clients, and tracking progress.
*   **Task Management:** Create tasks within projects, assign them to team members (if applicable), and monitor their status.
    *   *Task Status Tracking:* Use customizable statuses (e.g., To Do, In Progress, Completed).
    *   *Kanban-Style View:* Visualize task progress on a Kanban board.
*   *Billable Tasks & Projects:* Easily invoice clients for time spent on tasks and projects.

## VII. Reporting & Analytics

*   **Comprehensive Financial Reports:** Gain insights into your business performance with a suite of reports.
    *   *Invoice & Payment Reports:* Track revenue, outstanding payments, and payment history.
    *   *Expense Reports:* Analyze your spending patterns.
    *   *Profit & Loss Statement:* Understand your business's profitability.
    *   *Client & Sales Reports:* Identify your most valuable clients and sales trends.
    *   *Tax Summaries:* Simplify tax preparation with summarized tax information.
*   *Data Export:* Export your report data to CSV format for further analysis or use in other tools.
*   *Email Reports:* Schedule or send reports directly to your email.

## VIII. Customization & Settings

*   **Branding & Personalization:** Customize the look and feel of your invoices, quotes, and client portal to match your brand.
    *   *Multiple Invoice & Quote Templates:* Choose from various professional designs.
    *   *Logo & Company Information:* Display your branding prominently.
*   *Email Template Customization:* Tailor the content of emails sent to your clients.
*   *Tax Rate & Settings Configuration:* Define multiple tax rates and configure how they are applied.
*   *Payment Term Definition:* Set up standard payment terms for your business.
*   *User Management & Permissions:* (For multi-user setups) Manage users and control their access to features.
*   *Localization & Currency:* Support for multiple languages and currencies to cater to a global client base.
*   *Custom Fields:* Add custom fields to invoices, clients, or other records to capture specific information relevant to your business.

## IX. Platform & Integrations

*   **API for Custom Integrations:** Utilize the developer API to connect Invoice Ninja with other business applications.
*   *Webhooks:* Set up webhooks to receive real-time notifications of events (e.g., invoice paid, quote approved).
*   *Data Import/Export:* Easily import existing data or export your information for backup or migration. (Supports CSV, JSON, and specific platforms like QuickBooks, FreshBooks).
*   *Self-Hosting & Docker:* Option for users to host the application on their own infrastructure, including Docker support for easy deployment.
    *   *Self-Update Mechanism:* For self-hosted instances, an easy way to update to the latest version.
*   *Mobile & Desktop Applications:* Access your Invoice Ninja account on the go with dedicated mobile (iOS, Android) and desktop (Windows, macOS, Linux) apps.
*   *Bank Account Integration:* Connect bank accounts (via Yodlee, Nordigen) to help reconcile transactions.
*   *Zapier Integration (or similar via API/Webhooks):* Automate workflows by connecting Invoice Ninja to hundreds of other apps.
